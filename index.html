ConnectGreaterWashington Investment Proposals

Poplar Point

While this site is a feasible development proposal, it does not remain feasible under tests of economic stress due to current market conditions in the region. However, as investment in this site would catalyze investment in the region, it also faces significant potential for value growth in the long-term.

The Parks at Walter Reed

This project would reinvest in a neighborhood left behind by the departure of the Walter Reed Medical Center. While this is a strong investment, it does fold under certain economic pressures. Overall, it faces infrastructure alignment, equity impact, and stable economics.

ProjectConnect

This project is the strongest financially, remaining resilient under several economic pressures. It includes strong institiutional support from the University of Maryland and infrastrucure alignment from it's connections to the transit authority.

Conclusion

While Poplar Point does not generate the largest revenue, all of these projects are feasible and will generate profit from pursuing. The question remains therefore whether the additional profit of ProjectConnect or The Parks at Walter Reed is greater than the significant long-term investment benefits offered by Poplar Point. With the bonus to legacy and equity, support from local jurisdictions, and ability to spark catalytic invesment to gain potentially exponential returns, Poplar Point must be considered the prime investment opportunity.
